Who We Are

A Results-Driven Brand Communications Agency

AMITOUCH Global Concepts is a 360-degree Marketing and Brand Communications Agency that specializes in Above The Line (ATL) and Below The Line (BTL) marketing communications — delivering Strategy Development, Traditional and Digital Media, Experiential Marketing, and Event Management.

Our mission is to be the foremost 360-Degree brand and marketing agency in Nigeria, providing best-in-class services focused on results through excellent execution and profitability for business promoters across the African continent.

We address a critical market gap by providing customized brand marketing and sales activities that lead to actual sales — not just awareness. Sales and client satisfaction are the heart of everything we do.

ATL
Above The Line Marketing

Mass-market communications including Traditional Media (TV, Radio, Print, OOH), Digital Media campaigns, and Strategy Development that builds brand awareness at scale across Nigeria and Africa.

BTL
Below The Line Marketing

Targeted, direct marketing including Consumer Sampling, In-Store Sampling, Trade Activations, Road Shows, Sales Promotions, and Channel Development — activities that drive real purchase decisions.

EXP
Experiential Marketing

Immersive brand experiences — Product Launches, Brand Activations, Trade Shows, and Experiential Events that create emotional connections between consumers and brands, driving lasting loyalty.

360°
Full-Service Coverage

From strategy to execution — AMITOUCH is your one-stop brand and marketing partner. Events, Fabrications, Logistics, Building Branding, Foods and Catering, and end-to-end campaign management.
